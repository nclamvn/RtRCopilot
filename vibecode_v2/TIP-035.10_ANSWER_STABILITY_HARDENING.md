# TIP-035.10 — Answer Stability Hardening

## Header

- Date: 13/08/2026
- Depends on: TIP-035.9
- Trigger: TIP-034 capture on candidate `4065e93126b4`
- Priority: P0

## Context

The immutable TIP-034 campaign passed 25/27 but failed the minimum-domain
gate. `COM-008` returned a grounded answer that omitted a mandatory part of an
approved external reply. `SYS-003` returned a grounded draft whose prose did
not repeat the resolved MRP entity, so the final relevance gate correctly
rejected it. Both cases had passed on the same corpus/ontology digests in an
earlier capture, proving answer-shape variance rather than corpus drift.

## Requirements

- REQ-035.10-01: governed point facets must be able to declare material
  evidence-backed coverage checkpoints.
- REQ-035.10-02: missing governed checkpoints must be repaired locally from
  exact authorized evidence sentences, each carrying its original marker.
- REQ-035.10-03: an answer missing its canonical entity label may be anchored
  locally only when every missing target has a rendered marker bound to that
  target's governed records.
- REQ-035.10-04: entity anchoring must not override a conflicting/foreign
  entity mention, missing target evidence, grounding, authorization or DLP.
- REQ-035.10-05: deterministic stabilization must happen before a provider
  repair call, reducing variance, latency and spend without weakening gates.
- REQ-035.10-06: COM-008 and SYS-003 failure modes must have hermetic,
  domain-agnostic regressions; no external provider is called.
- REQ-035.10-07: the current candidate runtime and TIP-034 evidence remain
  historical; no rebuild, retry, reindex or connector operation occurs.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The company positioning facet enforces every component of its approved
   reply when that component is present in authorized evidence.
2. Broad MRP capability questions route to a governed capability facet with
   material planning/production/quality/forecasting checkpoints.
3. A grounded partial positioning answer is deterministically completed from
   the bound evidence and still passes strict sentence-level grounding.
4. A grounded MRP answer that omits the entity name gains only an approved
   canonical presentation label and becomes relevant without another provider
   call.
5. An answer backed by an unrelated record cannot gain an entity label.
6. Focused tests, backend suite, Ruff and mypy pass with zero network calls.

## Constraints

- Do not hard-code campaign IDs in production logic.
- Do not convert evaluator `required_terms` into runtime truth.
- Do not append ontology terms unless the same surface is present in the
  authorized evidence for the governed facet.
- Do not attach a marker to generated prose; appended factual content must be
  verbatim evidence units carrying their source marker.
- Do not retry TIP-033/TIP-034 or call an external LLM/embedding endpoint.

## Decision log

- The existing ontology profile/checkpoint mechanism is extended instead of
  adding question-specific branches.
- A canonical entity name is treated as governed presentation structure only
  after marker-to-record binding proves the answer subject.
- Local exact-evidence repair runs before the second LLM attempt. The provider
  remains responsible for synthesis; deterministic logic only restores
  curator-declared content that the provider omitted.
