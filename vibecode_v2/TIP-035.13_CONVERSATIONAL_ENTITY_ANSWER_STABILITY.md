# TIP-035.13 — Conversational Entity Answer Stability

## Header

- Date: 14/08/2026
- Depends on: TIP-035.12
- Trigger: TIP-034 capture on candidate `6044e19150e4`
- Priority: P0

## Context

The build-bound TIP-034 campaign passed 26/27 strict cases. `CHAT-004-T1`
(`CRM bên mình làm gì?`) returned `honest_null` with
`called_answer_irrelevant` after 35.7 seconds, while its follow-up resolved and
answered from `L3F.4.001`. The same case passed in prior captures on unchanged
corpus and ontology digests. This is answer-shape variance, not missing CRM
knowledge.

The existing deterministic stabilizer can add a missing canonical entity label
when a rendered citation marker is bound to that entity's governed records.
However, it rejected any other entity in the draft. A natural answer that said
`Real-time Robotics uses this system...` therefore treated the already-planned
company scope as a foreign entity and disabled stabilization.

## Requirements

- REQ-035.13-01: distinguish answer subjects from governed retrieval/scope
  entities without treating either as foreign to the query plan.
- REQ-035.13-02: a missing answer subject may be anchored only when a rendered
  marker is bound to that subject's ontology-governed definition/profile
  records.
- REQ-035.13-03: any resolved entity outside the complete query plan must keep
  the repair fail-closed.
- REQ-035.13-04: the rule must be generic across enterprise systems; no CRM,
  campaign ID, question string or evaluator required-term branch in production.
- REQ-035.13-05: conversational follow-ups must retain their ontology-bound
  subject, definition records and standalone question through external planning.
- REQ-035.13-06: unresolved HRM shorthand must never drift into product HERA;
  HRM remains a declared corpus/connector gap until authoritative onboarding.
- REQ-035.13-07: successful stabilization happens inside the first answer
  attempt and therefore avoids the 35-second repair/retry tail.
- REQ-035.13-08: no external provider, embeddings, reindex, connector,
  production operation or certification campaign is allowed in this TIP.

## Acceptance criteria

1. CRM, MRP and IP drafts that name the planned company scope but omit the
   canonical system label become relevant only through marker-to-record proof.
2. A CRM draft that names HERA remains irrelevant even if its marker is forged
   against the CRM record key.
3. Unbound evidence cannot add any canonical entity label.
4. The CRM pronoun follow-up keeps `RtR CRM`, `L3F.4.001` and the standalone
   question in its plan.
5. A full AnswerService integration path returns grounded in one provider call.
6. Focused, full backend, integration, Ruff and mypy gates pass locally.

## Constraints

- Do not weaken final answer relevance to lexical overlap alone.
- Do not infer a subject from answer prose or from an LLM-only proposal.
- Do not attach citations to generated factual prose.
- Canonical labels remain presentation structure; factual claims remain
  sentence-level grounded.
- Do not add HRM truth or aliases before an authoritative source, owner and
  export contract are approved.

## Decision log

- `query_plan.answer_entities` remains the final subject contract.
- `query_plan.entities` is the complete governed identity envelope for the
  turn, including owner/scope entities used by retrieval.
- Only identities outside that complete envelope are foreign and disable local
  anchoring.
- Marker-to-record binding, grounding, authorization, DLP and relevance remain
  unchanged and fail-closed.
