operation/path, phase, generation/context, edge, microsecond timestamp, outcome
and error class. No free text/content/SQL/actor/token identifiers. All normal,
error/cancel/incomplete representations use the same fixed frame size.

Closed nominal plans admit at most 32/30/8/30 frames for admission/submit/status/
queued cancel. Four hundred slots per operation give an exactly reachable worst
case of **40,000 frames**, not the looser 51,200 capacity. Full in-memory maximum
packet was constructed and parsed: 40,000 × 32 = 1,280,000 event bytes, plus
manifest/header 177, slot vector 1,607, fault 14 and trailer 12 = **1,281,810 bytes**.
Framing, maximum field widths, partial/error states and reserved fault bytes count.
Overflow/duplicate/unknown topology rejects the packet without mutating, trimming
or deduplicating the supplied records; unpaired entries remain explicitly incomplete.

This proves observer-wire size ONLY. Original journal/final JSON, stderr/stdout,
process/owner/supervisor and source/contract/closure maxima remain unproved.
Historical R21 size and a proposed 2 MiB reserve are not future bounds. Polling
the immutable supervisor's 10 MiB cap can overshoot; post-hoc STOP is not ex ante
complete-fit proof. Machine budget keeps original bounds false, total unavailable
