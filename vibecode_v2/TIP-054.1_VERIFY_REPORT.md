## Retained boundaries and next gates

- Worker loss uses conservative actionable failure and explicit bounded retry,
  not automatic replay of uncertain ingestion effects. Expiry is materialized
  by an active/returning poller, not an autonomous database timer.
- Uncertain older staged objects remain quarantined/unpublished. Retention-aware
  reconciliation/cleanup is still needed; do not infer safe garbage collection.
- Sequential processing and synchronous rendering can delay heartbeat/work.
  Database fencing prevents stale publication but does not forcibly preempt
  synchronous code. Renderer isolation and throughput belong to later work.
- Stop/drain all pre-0016 workers before upgrading. Empty-fixture rollback
  does not prove mixed-version rolling compatibility or active-job downgrade.
- Direct search, Intelligence and document binding lack a caller-selected
  `use_class` contract. ACL/provenance remain, but these are not attestation-
  certified surfaces. TIP-054.2/.3 must address purpose before new generalized
  contexts/actions consume them for narrower purposes.
- No real provider quality, serving latency/load, real company corpus,
  connector effects, production drills or employee evaluation was certified.
  Provider simulation is explicitly local and finite.

Existing business containers were untouched. Verification removed only its
20 owned disposable test containers and five owned test networks, including
synthetic volumes. Those disposable states are not retained; execution/failure
ledgers and the prepatch proof remain. Cached public QA tooling remains.
