## Decisions log

The Homeowner requested larger batches with fewer interruptions. Therefore internal
scan, implementation, controls, diagnosis, repair and verification are grouped into
one package; separate microtask approval/report checkpoints are removed. Technical
evidence gates and material authority boundaries remain. This changes workflow,
not architectural commitments or historical certification.

Implementation decision: preserve R26 and create a new versioned isolated adapter
with an explicit minimal root/format-identity diff. Connect real local subprocess
pipes and owned file/copy/journal endpoints, keeping unattached channels HOLD.
Existing host and backend-venv Python lack SQLAlchemy/Pydantic and test/style tools;
no install is needed for the stdlib local scope. Hash/AST-bound extraction of the
exact `RequestConnection.check_owner` and `join_terminal` methods may test a source
subset with real awaits and repeated cancellation. It is not a full application,
SQL, physical lease or universal scheduling-equivalence test. Do not mask original
BaseException/cancellation with recorder faults, or replace/replay terminal work.
The exact source-bound `latency_probe.main.retain` subset may likewise connect to
an owned journal endpoint without importing or executing the ASGI/SQL driver.
Subprocess stdout/stderr must drain concurrently within explicitly bounded logical
record domains; chunk splitting cannot manufacture domain compliance. Unknown or
unconnected channels remain HOLD even if zero-emission receipts seal a local packet.
