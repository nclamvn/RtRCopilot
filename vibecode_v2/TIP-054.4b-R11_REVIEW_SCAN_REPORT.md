## Design challenge / required boundaries

1. **Original work is not recorder overhead.** Timing the full wrappers returned
   by `PoolBoundaryObserver.sync/asynchronous`, `AsyncClient.request`,
   `AuditRepository.append`, or owner terminal methods includes actual SQL,
   driver/queue waits, product work or cleanup. Such envelopes must retain their
   original labels, not become observer-only cost. Candidate preparation hooks
   are known synchronous observer callbacks (`recording`, predicate/captured-ID,
   weak-ID, begin/finish, stack/pending/wake detail work). `PhaseProfiler.finish_phase`
   and synchronous observer serialization/write/flush are observer work, but
   include filesystem/preemption exposure, not isolated CPU.
2. **Measurement has a cost and can recurse.** New ID/stack/append operations must
   not instrument themselves through the same old WeakIds/recording hooks.
   Explicitly exclude/reentrancy-guard the NEW collector; capture bounded numeric
   identity outside the timed preparation region and disclose excluded work.
   New recorder exposure remains partial, not an overhead-free reference clock.
3. **Nesting is nonadditive.** An old begin/finish contains weak-ID/request work;
   audit emit contains serialization/flush. Use local parent IDs and half-open
   interval unions/masks, not sums of inclusive costs or separately ranked p95s.
   Global scheduling/cross-holder coupling and untimed observer work remain
   unknown. A small measured own-request span does not establish small total
   perturbation or exclude systemic queue effects.
4. **Post-inner-HTTP is expected, not unknown late work.** PhaseProfiler sets
   `request.closed=True` before phase finalization, JSON/write/flush, and resets
   the active ContextVar only afterward. Capture that original request key and
   label expected finalization explicitly. Terminal children can inherit the
   original context; never resolve an ended span using mutable latest owner/task
   state. Missing association stays unknown/rejected, not assigned to another
   request. Separate R11/R9/phase/audit/PG numeric namespaces.
5. **Reference timing/order is immutable.** Measure actual original delegation
   once. Do not skip, defer or reorder its finalize/serialize/write/flush/return
   sequence; buffer only NEW numeric records. A broad `json.dumps` monkeypatch
   is not a scoped serialization hook: prefer module-local delegation/proxy with
   strict target classification and exact restoration. A stream proxy must
   preserve original argument, return and exception, without parsing/storing
   line content, and keep original flush synchronous.
6. **Recording must not affect outcomes.** Fault containment covers only NEW
   synchronous recording. Original delegate/await/error/cancellation must be
   outside that containment, with no retry, callback await or added SQL. Cleanup
   children must finish their original owned terminal work; observer finalization
   must not replace a primary exception. Wrap raw class/static/inherited
   descriptors with their actual binding and restore absence as absence.
7. **No lifetime or identity invention.** Numeric/weak maps and detached records
   must not retain Task/Future/Connection/Session/driver objects or reactivate
   old facade generations. Record the actual captured departing resource/task,
   not the owner's subsequently live generation. Queue wake association is
   availability, not reservation or a unique delivered-record handoff.
