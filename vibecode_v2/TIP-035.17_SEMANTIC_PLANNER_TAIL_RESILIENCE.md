# TIP-035.17 — Semantic Planner Tail Resilience

## Header

- Date: 14/08/2026
- Depends on: TIP-035.16 external recertification on candidate `9113113b32c2`
- Module: Enterprise Query Intelligence
- Priority: P1

## Context

The build-bound TIP-034 campaign answered 25/27 cases. PRD-013 and OPS-008
both stopped safely before retrieval after almost exactly 20 seconds with
`not_called_semantic_planner_required`. The corpus, ontology and database were
bound and unchanged; both questions already contained enough governed
structure to produce an immutable semantic contract:

- PRD-013 names both exact comparison subjects, while each subject has
  authoritative ontology record bindings.
- OPS-008 names one exact governed business program and asks for its explicit
  approval/policy state, also covered by authoritative bindings.

The defect is therefore planner tail coupling, not missing knowledge. A slow
external semantic planner can currently veto questions for which it cannot add
identity, authority or answer-shape information.

## Requirements

- REQ-035.17-01: bypass the external semantic planner when a comparison has at
  least two exact, unambiguous ontology identities and every identity has an
  authoritative definition binding.
- REQ-035.17-02: bypass the planner for a point lifecycle/governance-state
  lookup only when exactly one exact governed identity is independently bound
  to authoritative records.
- REQ-035.17-03: state vocabulary alone must never establish identity or permit
  retrieval.
- REQ-035.17-04: an unresolved comparison side, ambiguous identity,
  conversation-dependent question or missing record binding must retain the
  external planner and fail-closed behavior.
- REQ-035.17-05: deterministic language remains authoritative for intent,
  completeness and answer shape; the fast lane must not introduce case IDs,
  question strings or evaluator terms into production logic.
- REQ-035.17-06: successful fast-lane plans retain normal ACL, DLP, retrieval,
  citation, grounding and answer verification gates.
- REQ-035.17-07: no external call, corpus mutation, embeddings, reindex,
  connector or production operation is allowed in this TIP.

## Acceptance criteria

1. An exact HERA/VEGA comparison resolves deterministically, binds both
   entities and their records, and makes zero planner calls.
2. An exact maintenance-program state question resolves deterministically,
   includes `L3F.2.010`, and makes zero planner calls.
3. A comparison with an unresolved side still invokes the planner; failure
   produces `planner_source=fallback` and `planning_required`.
4. A generic state question without an exact governed subject still invokes
   the planner and cannot acquire an entity from state vocabulary.
5. Existing exact definition, collection, ambiguity and conversation planning
   behavior remains unchanged.
6. Focused tests, full backend tests, Ruff and mypy pass locally.

## Constraints

- Do not raise the 20-second timeout as the remediation.
- Do not accept an LLM-only entity or broad similarity candidate as proof.
- Do not weaken `quality_planner_missing` for unresolved plans.
- Do not weaken answer grounding or citation verification.
- Do not call external providers during BUILD or VERIFY.

## Decision log

- The resilience mechanism is a zero-network deterministic fast lane, not a
  permissive post-timeout downgrade.
- Eligibility requires three independent proofs: explicit linguistic
  structure, exact governed identity and authoritative record binding.
- Conversation context disables this fast lane because reference resolution
  may still require semantic planning.
- The workflow is shortened to SCAN → TIP → BUILD → VERIFY; no architecture
  checkpoint is needed because the approved trust boundary is unchanged.
