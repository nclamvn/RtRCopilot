## Task and specifications

initial persisted read; mutation in the original UnitOfWork; fresh persisted
postcommit disclosure read. Keep a request-local owning Task and once-only
mutation/outcome state, with generation-local session/connection guards. Never
close an active transaction, acquire while uncertain cleanup remains, reuse a
stale handle, replay after mutation starts, fabricate a postcommit result, cache
authority, or reset the existing execution timeout. Preserve maintenance/input/
evidence/audit/fresh-authority order, actual commit, atomic audit, idempotency,
deadline clocks and unsupported/clock/R1/legacy whole-path fallback.

Product scope: private `actions/request_connection.py`, `actions/service.py`,
`actions/execution.py` only. No shared engine, UOW, audit, schema, API, DTO, pool,
dependency, timeout or quota change. Preserve unrelated dirty work.
