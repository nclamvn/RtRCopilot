Required design safeguards:

- Leave identity-gated `ActionPolicy.current`, factory types/options, service
  origin and pool unchanged. Wrapping `current` changes its `__func__` identity
  and silently selects legacy status; such an observer would invalidate comparison.
  Prove topology/path equivalence before interpreting timings, including custom
  origins/fallbacks and cancel's ordinary Session contexts.
- Preserve every post-wait/commit authority check, idempotency/receipt/deadline/
  budget/cancel history and joined cleanup. No extra await/checkout/TX, source
  content, SQL text, token, authority cache or replay. Restore exact patched seams
  on every terminal/error path; serving stays OFF.
- Label acquisition as a composite (queue/pre-ping/connect/scheduling), not pure
  pool wait. Distinguish nested audit/checkpoint spans and reentrant acquires;
  use interval unions/residuals, not sums of inclusive spans or percentiles.
- Falsify dominance claims: small acquisition rejects acquisition-dominant
  explanation; small mutation/audit region rejects audit-tail dominance; small
  post-commit/terminal regions reject cleanup dominance. Mixed/nested signals can
  remain unresolved; capture cannot force a sole-cause conclusion.

  relax caps or repeat the earlier observer-cap fault. Reject the future admission
