## OUTCOME

Ask is now a conversation-first workspace. The answer stream occupies the
primary canvas, the most recent question appears as a restrained user turn,
and the composer is anchored to the bottom through layout rows rather than an
overlay. On wide screens, related processing, source and session context stay
visible in a right rail; on laptop/mobile they move into an explicit drawer.

  contextual chips, pop-up advanced scope and circular send action.

  research and reopened-conversation states in the scrollable thread.
