## Outcome

The semantic planner no longer owns the latency tail for questions whose
meaning is already fully proven by deterministic inputs. Two generic
zero-network lanes were added:

1. comparisons with at least two exact, unambiguous ontology subjects where
   every subject has authoritative record bindings;
2. lifecycle/governance-state lookups with exactly one exact governed subject
   and authoritative record bindings.

Both lanes preserve deterministic intent, completeness and answer shape. They
then continue through the unchanged ACL, DLP, retrieval, citation, grounding
and answer-verification pipeline. The external planner is not called because
it cannot add identity, authority or structural meaning to these contracts.

## Failure closed

The change does not convert a planner timeout into a permissive fallback.
Questions remain on the external/fail-closed path when any of the following is
true:

- a comparison side is unresolved;
- an identity is ambiguous, fuzzy/partial or lacks authoritative bindings;
- the question depends on conversation history;
- state vocabulary is present without an exact governed subject.

No TIP case ID, evaluator term or full question string exists in production
logic.
