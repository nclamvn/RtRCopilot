# TIP-054.0 · Enterprise OS research and Blueprint

Date: 2026-10-06 · priority: P0 · phase: decision preparation.
Baseline: `0e88f48588dc3228f1ae9eca7a7597d96589af8b`.
Dependencies: current application source and TIP-053 baseline; no deployment.

## Objective

Prepare a reproducible, source-backed upgrade contract for RtR Copilot to
become a useful Enterprise Knowledge & Action OS. Preserve the existing
specialist services and distinguish a research recommendation from measured
runtime suitability. The first implementation target is a reliable internal
draft workflow, not unrestricted autonomous business writes.

## Scope and Builder assignments

The Contractor uses Vibecode Kit v6.2 for scan, condensed five-persona RRI,
Vision, Blueprint and dependency graph. Refinery v1.3 governs public research.

- Read-only Builder: inspect current source, reusable contracts and gaps.
- Research Builder: fixed public cohort, frozen raw snapshots, exact evidence,
  honest-null registry and reproducible/offline adversarial checks.
- Acceptance Builder: synthetic scenarios, fixtures and structural verifier;
  never manufacture executed E2E, model scores or employee feedback.
- Contractor: independently inspect deliverables, compare alternatives,
  prepare the architecture/requirements/task graph, and verify the packet.

## Product requirements and constraints

The product requirement matrix is [SCAN_AND_REQUIREMENTS](../docs/enterprise-os/SCAN_AND_REQUIREMENTS.md).
This TIP completes preparation, not those 32 application requirements.
Architecture is [BLUEPRINT](../docs/enterprise-os/BLUEPRINT.md); implementation
sequence is [TASK_GRAPH](../docs/enterprise-os/TASK_GRAPH.md).

R0 reads and explicitly requested R1 internal drafts use existing rights and
intent without repetitive confirmations. R2/R3 effect permissions are exact,
version-bound and owner configured. Reuse SOT, PolicyEngine, Documents, Files,
audit and evidence identity. Do not rebuild a custom general orchestration
engine or invent a second authoritative company fact store.

No external model or embedding call, private-data crawl, actual connector
activation, production operation, product dependency install or runtime
rebuild is part of this preparation TIP. Public documentation remains outside
company SOT. Unknown performance, cost or license compatibility stays unknown.

## Protocol and approval seam

Submit the completed packet for a single Blueprint decision. Its approval
covers local TIP-054.1–054.9 implementation and synthetic testing, with reports
after coherent milestones. Existing internal-export, actual source/write and
production boundaries remain separate. Routine engineering decisions inside
the approved contract do not require a new approval each time.

Completion and Verify reports must state requirement coverage, AC scenarios,
technical health, issues/deviations and the precise READY scope. The serving
application is not certified by structural research/catalog validation.
