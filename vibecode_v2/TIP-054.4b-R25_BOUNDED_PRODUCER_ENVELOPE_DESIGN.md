## Fidelity and limits

No silent truncation, dropping errors, repairing missing descendants or swapping
finite codes for raw diagnostics while still calling the packet lossless. A
proposed normalized error contract must state what is retained, what cannot be
represented and what makes evidence unqualified. Raw data exceeding supported
domains must force INCOMPLETE/STOP, never successful capture. A digest of omitted
data is not preservation. Failed terminal persistence/crash must not manufacture
a guaranteed durable STOP; without intact final proof, completeness is false.

Disk occupancy and measurement completeness are separate. A quota may bound an
incomplete artifact but cannot prove original unrestricted output fits. Finite
proposed allocations/reserves are admission rules, not producer proofs. State
precisely what existing sources would require a future owned adapter/contract
change and what would need fidelity/async/path/restoration verification. No claim
that finite unrestricted diagnostics, unchanged sources and finite cap coexist.
