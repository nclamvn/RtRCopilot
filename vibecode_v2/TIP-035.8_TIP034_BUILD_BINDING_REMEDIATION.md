# TIP-035.8 — TIP-034 Build-Binding Remediation

## Header

- Date: 13/08/2026
- Depends on: TIP-035.7
- Priority: P0

## Task

Give the TIP-034 wrapper the same immutable candidate-binding contract as
TIP-033, verify it without external calls, commit and push the remediation,
then rebuild primary and isolated pilot on the new exact candidate.

## Requirements

- REQ-035.8-01: derive the candidate from `scripts/build-identity.sh`.
- REQ-035.8-02: reject dirty, placeholder or otherwise non-immutable identity.
- REQ-035.8-03: export `E2E_BUILD_IDENTITY` before the enterprise E2E runner.
- REQ-035.8-04: retain the immutable 27-case scope, concurrency 1 and browser-off
  defaults.
- REQ-035.8-05: tests must use a local stub runner and perform zero external
  calls.
- REQ-035.8-06: rebuild primary and pilot on the resulting commit and verify
  exact runtime build, schema, canonical corpus and service health.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A clean 12–64 hexadecimal build identity reaches the runner unchanged.
2. A dirty `sha+digest` identity stops before the runner.
3. A modified case set stops before the runner.
4. Local lint/type/unit and wrapper tests pass without Docker/LLM activity.
5. The committed SHA is pushed to `main`; primary and pilot runtime verifiers
   return that exact SHA.

## Constraints

- Do not rerun TIP-034 under the exhausted TIP-035.7 authorization.
- Do not call an external LLM, embeddings endpoint, connector or reindex job.
- Do not modify the governed human observation ledger.
- Do not stage `_to_delete/`.
