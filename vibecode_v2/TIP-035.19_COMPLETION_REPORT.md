## Outcome

The generic answer pipeline now recovers a useful single-subject answer when a
provider draft is grounded to the right record but renders an irrelevant
entity shape. Recovery is deterministic and bounded: it uses only a marker
that the provider both declared and rendered, requires that marker to resolve
to the planned subject's ontology-bound definition/profile record, and emits
only the governed canonical subject label plus verbatim evidence units.

The recovered answer re-enters the unchanged relevance, grounding,
completeness, profile coverage, authorization, DLP, lineage and persistence
gates. Successful recovery takes one provider call and records
`answer_irrelevant_extractive_recovery` in the append-only audit trail.

## Root cause closed

`CHAT-004-T1` did not fail because CRM knowledge was absent. The approved CRM
record and ontology binding were present, but a grounded provider draft could
carry a foreign entity shape. The prior canonical-label stabilizer correctly
refused to edit that ambiguous draft, after which the retry path could still
end in honest-null. TIP-035.19 replaces that unusable draft with its own exact,
selected, subject-bound source instead of trusting or paraphrasing its prose.

## Safety properties

- No campaign ID, question literal, CRM-specific branch or evaluator term is
  present in production logic.
- Unselected, unbound, stale, pending or foreign-entity evidence is rejected.
- Multi-subject and exhaustive contracts cannot use this recovery lane.
- Failed recovery retains the existing fail-closed repair/honest-null path.
- The local recovery reason is persisted even though no second provider call
  is made.
