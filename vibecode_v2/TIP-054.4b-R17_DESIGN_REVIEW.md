## Finite campaign and interpretation

The eight cells `D-legacy,D-new,M-new,M-legacy,M-legacy,M-new,D-new,D-legacy`
contain100 scheduled calls each. Each lane/mode occurs twice/200 calls and its
absolute cell positions sum to9. Each cell preserves receipt0/2/100 populations
34/33/33. This balances absolute sequence position within one fixed chronology;
it is neither randomization nor independent causal replication. Preserve both
individual cells and combined mode/lane/receipt distributions so aggregation
cannot hide order-specific tails. Duplicate lane/mode cells require distinct
cell-bearing attempt IDs, not the old c25/lane/ordinal identity alone.

One ten-slot warm pass leaves five idle base slots; subsequent overflow creation
is timed. No cell rewarm, cold/warm tuning, retry, replacement or second campaign
is allowed. Schedule/start/outcome conservation must cover unstarted and
started-missing calls, with null missing latency. All observed errors/timeouts
belong in primary elapsed distributions; success-only views remain secondary.
The unchanged1000ms/error0/missing0 diagnostic gate may fail honestly without
reclassifying measured chronology as root cause or removing evidence.

The acquisition interval is composite, even when a nested connect interval is
observed. Creation origin and chronology can discriminate some lifecycle cases,
but subtraction cannot identify pure queue/pre-ping/CPU overhead. Query, hold,
acquisition and terminal spans overlap; do not add overlapping spans or subtract
percentiles. Event-loop lag is sampled scheduling evidence, not a host CPU cause
or per-request scheduler-cost estimate. Minimal/detailed differences describe
incremental exposure under fixed order, not total baseline observer cost.
