phase-owned physical leases is the strongest narrow comparator. Release initial
only after its real rollback/close; use original UOW mutation on a separate
lease; release only after actual commit/rollback; obtain a third lease for the

initial-to-mutation occupancy is the relevant segment. It still retains the slot

only after the stronger three-phase comparator, not as assumed savings.
