## Regression hardening

The data-integration gate exposed a query-intelligence regression unrelated to
the Office glyph symptom: `timeout` could be interpreted as the `time` segment
of Real-time Robotics. The matcher now accepts only a registered alias part
that begins with the user's token. This preserves useful shorthand such as
`Omni` → `OmniSight` while preventing a longer compound word from triggering a
company entity. The new regression test passes in the final backend suite.

## Final decision

TIP-026.3 is accepted for local pilot testing. The reported document and the
entire current Office corpus have been recovered. Future unsupported fonts now
fail closed instead of degrading into a visually plausible but corrupted
preview.
